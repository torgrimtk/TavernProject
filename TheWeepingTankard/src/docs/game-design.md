# The Weeping Tankard 

## Vision

A cozy, murky and grim tavern management game, where people of all calibers go to drink, share secrets and meet people.

## Player fantasy

The player should feel like they are: 
- Running a magical, dark and cozy tavern
- Meets new characters
- Running and growing a small business

## Core gameplay loop

1. Customers arrive
2. Player serves the customer
3. Player earns gold
4. Player upgrades tavern
5. The player can also interact with the NPCs
6. A customer's patience timer begins when their order is placed. Their patience decreases while they're waiting for that order. Once their drink is delivered, the patience timer stops.

## MVP Features 
- Tavern room
- Customers/NPC's
- Orders
- Gold
- Basic upgrades (such as..?)
